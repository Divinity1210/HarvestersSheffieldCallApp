1. Introduction

"Hello, may I speak with [Name]?"

"Hi [Name], my name is [Your Name], and I’m calling from Harvesters Birmingham. I hope you’re doing well."

"I’m calling to specially invite you to Awakening, our two day gathering focused on breakthrough and spiritual renewal. It’s going to be a powerful time of prayer, worship and the word, and we would really love for you to be part of it."

📍 Venue Park Regis, 160 Broad St, Birmingham B15 1DT 🗺️ Open in Google Maps →
🗓️ Dates Sat 26th & Sun 27th Sept
🕙 Time 09:00am - 03:00pm
🚗 Parking Free parking and on-street parking available
2. Engage (The Ask)

"Will you be able to join us?"

If they say YES:
"That’s great! We look forward to welcoming you. Feel free to invite your family and friends too."
If they’re UNSURE:
"No problem at all. I just wanted to personally invite you and make sure you’re aware of it. We’d love to have you with us if you’re able to make it, even if it’s just for one day."
If they say NO:
"That’s absolutely fine. Thank you for your time, and we hope to welcome you at one of our upcoming services."
3. Closing Blessing

"Thank you for your time. Have a lovely day, and God bless you."
