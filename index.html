📋 Call Script (Sunday Celebration) ▼
1. Introduction

"Hello, may I speak with [Name]?"

"Hi [Name], my name is [Your Name], and I’m calling from Harvesters Croydon. I hope you’re having a blessed week!"

📍 Venue The Legacy Centre, 14 Imperial Way, Croydon CR0 4RR 🗺️ Open in Google Maps →
🗓️ Dates & Time Every Sunday · 10:00 AM
🚗 Parking Free on-site parking available
2. The Ask / Invitation

"Will you and your family be able to join us in church this Sunday?"

If they say YES:
"That is wonderful! We look forward to welcoming you. Feel free to invite your family and friends too — parking is free on-site!"
If they are UNSURE:
"No problem at all! I just wanted to personally connect and let you know you're in our prayers. Can I WhatsApp you the details, and is there any prayer request we can stand with you in this week?"
If they say NO:
"That's completely fine! Thank you so much for your time. You can also join our livestream online, and we pray that God's grace abounds for you throughout the week."
3. Closing Blessing

"Thank you for your time. Have a blessed, fruitful week ahead, and God bless you!"
